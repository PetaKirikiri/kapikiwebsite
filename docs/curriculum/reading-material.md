## Vocabulary relationship

Basis: the proposed groups in the Connectors workspace's `docs/curriculum/vocabulary-progression.draft.json`, checked 29 September 2026. That file is still a partial editorial draft, not an approved 1,000-word syllabus. The readings intentionally reuse earlier vocabulary. Their word-occurrence totals are reading length, never new words mastered.

The following are present in these readings but are not allocated at or before that level in the draft. They need to be included or deliberately introduced as supporting language when the vocabulary distribution is finalised. Personal/place names are listed for completeness, not for the target vocabulary count. Inflected forms and particles are not automatically separate counted vocabulary items.

- Level 2: Rotorua, mutunga, o, tōku, Pōneke, taku, tēpu, mō.
- Level 3: a, Maia, Hana, horoia, tangohia, rite, katoa.
- Level 4: tā, tō, ruma, Maia, hou, Hana.
- Level 5: o, hou, tawhito, Hana, whakatōngia, Maia, tupu, inā.
- Level 6: a, Maia, Hana, o, mea, whakatō, oti, taua, wā, ā, tae.

These lists identify editorial decisions; they are not a second vocabulary store. No canonical structures, curriculum assignments, Floor Plans, learned words or learner progress are written by the reading component.

## Presentation

Reuses Level 1's card and read-only sentence renderer. The complete Māori reading and English support appear immediately, even while course data or grammar analysis is unavailable. Each sentence uses the existing read-only analysis endpoint independently; there are at most two simultaneous requests, cancelled on leaving the reading. No component-owned POS lists or fabricated rails are introduced. Unknown grammar remains plain text.
